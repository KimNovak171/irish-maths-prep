Privacy at Irish Maths Prep

Last updated: 11 August 2026.

We designed the First Class Maths Skills Snapshot to collect as little information as possible.

What the free snapshot asks for

The snapshot does not ask for your child's name, date of birth, school, photograph, home address or exact location.

Maths responses

The browser uses the child's responses to calculate the on-screen skills snapshot. For validation and product improvement, we may record anonymous or pseudonymous usage events such as starting or completing the snapshot and broad skill areas selected for practice. We do not need a child's identity for this.

Parent email

If you voluntarily request a practice plan or product updates, we collect the parent email address you provide and the minimum information needed to personalise that request. We use it only for the purpose described at the point of collection and related Irish Maths Prep updates where you have consented.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. You can also ask us to delete the information associated with your email address.

Independent tool

Irish Maths Prep is an independent parent resource. It is not an official NCCA assessment or endorsement.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: hello@irishmathsprep.com.

This validation-stage privacy notice should be reviewed again before the product expands its data collection, accounts, payments or child profiles.
